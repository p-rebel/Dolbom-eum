Korean and English Tesseract language models

The files in tessdata-v4 are the integer LSTM models (4.0.0_best_int) distributed as
@tesseract.js-data/kor and @tesseract.js-data/eng, version 1.0.0.
Source models: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best
Package sources: https://github.com/naptha/tessdata
